# Mobile Checklist

## Required Viewports

- Phone: around `360x800`
- Tablet: around `768x1024`
- Desktop: around `1280x800` or wider

## Checks

- No horizontal overflow in normal page use
- Headings, body text, labels, and helper copy remain readable without zooming
- Primary actions stay easy to find and tap
- Navigation, tables, charts, and forms do not collapse into clutter
- Dialogs and drawers fit inside the viewport and scroll safely
- Buttons and icon actions remain large enough for touch
- Error states are visible, not only announced indirectly
- Background imagery does not overpower form readability
- Empty states still communicate the next useful action
